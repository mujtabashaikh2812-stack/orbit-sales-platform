# Orbit 🪐
### AI Sales Outreach & CRM Platform

> Keep your high-value prospects, customer relationships, and deal momentum in your company's natural gravitational pull.

---

## 🎯 Overview
**Orbit** is a modern, AI-native sales platform that bridges the gap between cold outreach and long-term customer relationship management. 

### Core Capabilities
- **📡 Signal-Driven Prospecting**: Identify high-intent leads based on buying triggers rather than bulk spam.
- **✍️ Hyper-Personalized Outreach**: AI-crafted emails and multi-channel touches that sound authentic and contextual.
- **🔄 Autonomous Cadences**: Automated follow-ups, reply classification, and meeting scheduling.
- **📊 Unified CRM Pipeline**: Track deal stages, contact history, account health, and pipeline velocity.
- **🧠 Relationship Intelligence**: AI insights into prospect sentiment, deal risk, and next best actions.
